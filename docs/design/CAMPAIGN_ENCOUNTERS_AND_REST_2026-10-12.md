# Campaign encounter footing and rest-site clarity

## [Codex | 2026-10-12] Grounded implementation plan

Oliver authorized another round of core campaign improvements after v2.182.0. A muted browser inspection of the 16 authored campaign halves found ground enemies with stale vertical coordinates on stairs, two Winding Cliffs enemies beyond their paths, an unsupported Castle Gates caster, and a repeating Broken Walls caster intersecting scenery. Lost Canyon's south guards inherit heights from nearby overhead geometry because generic spawning selects the highest surface before the authored layout finishes. Many initial-height findings can settle during play; distinguish those from permanent unsupported or blocked starts in the tests.

Correct off-path authored coordinates explicitly. Add a bounded, deterministic encounter-footing pass after campaign construction, preserving intended floor height, enemy identity, counts, roles, story ownership and patrol membership. It can adjust height or move within 96 world units to obtain supported feet and torso clearance; never jump to a different room, choose a distant upper floor or delete a failed enemy. Update repeating patrol slots to use the corrected point. Keep boss, flying, tutorial, procedural-mode and authored spiral-storey behavior outside this correction. Record unresolved positions for QA rather than hiding them.

Existing healing pads provide 40% of maximum health per finite charge; Mara's restored large pad is unlimited. Their six cubes and ground cross communicate little about availability or regional setting. Rebuild their visual presentation as low, walk-through rest sites using a common readable health emblem, a visible charge arc and region-appropriate fittings: farm supplies/cloth, sandstone basins, fortress stone/iron, icy cairns, forge cooling bowls, coastal timber/brass, palace marble/gold and castle dark stone/silver. Keep silhouettes compact enough for existing path widths, reuse shared geometry/materials, add no collision or gameplay advantage, and retain the existing renderer fallback. Show a short contextual instruction near a reachable pad, including depleted, locked and unlimited states. Do not expose distant undiscovered rests through walls or add map-wide clutter.

Check the Lost Canyon cache and Thunder Cliffs lower lift brake against their actual approach paths before moving anything. Do not mistake the intentionally later-accessible Ice Caves freeze valve or Sky Library second crank for a missing route. Correct outdated new-player hints that claim only gold carries out of a campaign session.

Validation: unit cases for floor/headroom, nearby stairs, stacked floors, unsupported spawns and repeat patrol positions; real-browser comparison across all 16 halves, enemy approach/attack checks, actual nearby interaction and rest consumption; old-save, checkpoint and two-client coverage; normal-camera regional screenshots and shader errors. Human judgments of fun and difficulty remain open. Preserve current story, rewards, saves, brighter regional identity and the custom-SFX hold.
