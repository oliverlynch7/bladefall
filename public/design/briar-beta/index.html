What to judge when you play

Can you read the route without constant arrows? Does moving the crate make physical sense? Are the required jumps satisfying? Can you spot the braced posts in the boss arena and use one during its charge? Where does movement, waiting or dialogue become tedious?

Design notes and test boundaries

The Dev preview is solo and disposable. It keeps the approved mill, current two-chapter story, five shard routes and optional activities.
