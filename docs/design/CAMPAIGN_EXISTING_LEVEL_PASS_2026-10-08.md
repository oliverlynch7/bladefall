# Existing campaign level pass — 8 October 2026

Oliver prefers improving the live, full-scale campaign layouts to replacing them wholesale. The current Briar and Keep beta maps remain review references. This pass addresses specific contradictions and repeated errands found in the existing 16-part audit; it does not assert that the entire campaign is now well paced or human-playtested.

## Grounded findings and changes

1. **The Dungeons:** `paths.service` currently connects the lower ring to the upper shelter while the main quest says the prisoners need a lift. Remove this bypass and show the ruined service stair as broken architecture. Keep the lift and its existing weight/escort state. Once the prisoners are safe, the lift must cycle for a player who missed the first ascent, including a second co-op player. Verify lower/upper boarding, saved `kd.transfer`, exit reachability, and optional lower works.
2. **Thunder Cliffs:** `paths.ascent` already reaches the middle ledge and `tc.approach` unnecessarily requires `tc.lift`. Keep the cargo lift as a useful return shortcut. The required climb runs through the shackle and upper barrier. Update Abe, the journal and the tracker so the story matches the physical path. Verify progress with and without repairing the lift.
3. **Deep Ice Caves:** the player rescues Ellis, walks out to his dropped pack, then must walk back to the same NPC before they can operate the crossing. The notes themselves can show Emberdeep's arms route on pickup; the conversation about Ellis's role remains available. Preserve rescue, sealed notes, waterworks and officer gate, along with all existing flag IDs so old saves work. Verify a fresh path that never returns to Ellis, the optional conversation, and a prior-save path.
4. **Snowbound Peaks:** the required heater part currently sends the player from Heath's shelter west to the abandoned lift house and back. Keep the west lift house as an optional route and winter-cloak branch, but move a dislodged heater coil to the winch immediately ahead of Heath. Let the player fit it directly and watch the crossing lower. Heath remains a guide and can still help install an unspent coil for previous saves. Preserve the `ff.part` and `ff.heater` flags and verify both installation methods, optional west loop, wrong signal shape, and prior saves.
5. **Iron Halls:** the west stores already sit along a route toward the service cart, but the objective sends the player past them to meet Jack, then back for handles, then back to Jack. Let a player who finds the handles fit them into the cart at the cart itself. Jack remains helpful and can still do the repair for anyone who brings him the parts, including old saves. Keep the worker rescue and machine shutdown intact. Check both routes and that the cart's deck actually becomes traversable.
6. **Shipwreck Shore:** the rudder, sail and rope already come from different traversal, combat and cave routes, and the existing 3D boat model responds to installation flags. Add a single workbench interaction beside Otto that fits any carried parts, updates the boat model and marks it ready as soon as the third part is installed. Keep Otto's delivery dialogue as another way to fit parts, and auto-complete the boat in that route too. The main objective arrow must lead to the workbench when carrying a part, and the pickup notice must say where to fit it. Verify both paths, mixed installation order, the final boarding gate, and previous flags.

The objective arrows for Peaks, Iron Halls and Thunder Cliffs still encoded the older compulsory NPC/lift sequence after the quests changed. Their route targets must agree with the new forward path; otherwise the player will be sent back despite the repaired logic.

An early visual probe placed the player beyond Snowbound's unopened crossing, a location normal play cannot reach. The closed crossing filled that view as expected. A separate probe beneath Heath's shelter roof did expose a crowded normal camera. The shelter awning now clears from view when the player stands beneath it, and the elevated roof-shard slab clears only when the player is below it. The slab remains solid and visible when the player stands on top; this matters because disappearing parkour floors have been a recurring complaint.

## Acceptance and limits

- Inspect the actual inputs and visible consequences; these route adjustments do not count as new puzzle families.
- Check affected routes in a real muted browser, in normal camera as well as state tests. Include wrong action/recovery where a control remains.
- Keep quest IDs, shard IDs, save format and co-op authority stable. Preserve local unrelated edits.
- Continue the wider campaign audit after these verified fixes. Counts of encounters or props are not proof of pacing or fun; human playtesting remains the gate for that conclusion.

## Implementation review — 8 October 2026

The six route repairs above are in the live campaign code for version 2.143.0. The Keep stair is visibly broken, and its lift cycles again after the prisoners reach safety. Thunder Cliffs can be climbed without treating its cargo lift as a prerequisite. Ellis's pack reveals the Emberdeep lead on pickup. Snowbound's loose coil can be carried forward to the winch, while the western lift-house route and Heath's older installation path remain available. Iron Halls has a cart brake beside the parked cart and a short cart-roll presentation after repair. Shipwreck Shore has an Otto-side workbench; each fitted part changes the boat, and the final one opens boarding. Otto can still fit parts in conversation, and his crossing briefing remains optional.

Verification: static gate; all five affected level regressions; browser route, boat and two-player co-op scripts; a pre-change save load; and normal-camera screenshots of the winch, roof shard, cart and boat. Browser scripts reported no page exceptions. The roof shard remains solid and visible while standing on it. The cart repair currently uses a compact procedural visual that communicates motion but does not yet carry the art quality of a finished set-piece. Enemy pressure, travel-time boredom, puzzle satisfaction and broader visual coherence still require a human playthrough. This pass deliberately does not claim to solve every live map.
