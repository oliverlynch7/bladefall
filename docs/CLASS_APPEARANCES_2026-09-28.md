# Class appearance designs

All 17 classes retain their default. Rank 5 and 10 unlock the following cosmetic-only designs through the Stylist; selections persist per class.

| Class | Rank 5 | Rank 10 |
|---|---|---|
| warrior | **Crimson Officer** — Bronze shoulder plates and a short helmet crest. | **Royal Vanguard** — Cobalt armor, gold layered shoulders and a tall officer crest. |
| ranger | **Autumn Scout** — Copper leaf clasps and an autumn feather mantle. | **Whitewood Hunter** — Ivory and deep jade with a broad swept feather mantle. |
| mage | **Amethyst Scholar** — Amethyst robes and a crystal-set collar. | **Galaxy Archmage** — Midnight violet, a tilted celestial halo and orbiting stars. |
| reaper | **Ash Reaper** — Ash-silver bone ribs over burgundy cloth. | **Pale Harvester** — Pale bone collar, hooked ribs and hanging soul stones. |
| paladin | **Dawn Guardian** — Light gold armor and a radiant sun clasp. | **Radiant Champion** — Ivory and luminous gold with sculpted sunburst shoulders. |
| necromancer | **Grave Scholar** — Bone collar and jade spirit lanterns. | **Soul Shepherd** — Ivory ribs and two gently drifting green soul lanterns. |
| ninja | **Copper Shadow** — Copper-edged arm guards and layered shoulder blades. | **Night Fox** — Silver-blue armor, swept fox-ear crest and crossed back blades. |
| berserker | **Iron Fury** — Heavy bronze shoulder teeth. | **Wild Champion** — Bone and oxblood with huge uneven tusked pauldrons. |
| pirate | **Redcoat Captain** — Red coat, gold epaulettes and a compass medallion. | **Admiral of Tides** — Deep teal and wine-red with a captain crest and dangling compass chains. |
| chronomancer | **Clockmaker** — Brass collar, clock face and tiny side gears. | **Keeper of Hours** — Ivory-brass clockwork with a rotating chest dial and shoulder gears. |
