# Campaign foot-soldier melee reach — grounded fix

Oliver reports that ordinary combat is too easy and enemy attacks feel unthreatening. A muted-browser audit of isolated grunts and sentinels at 50, 72 and 110 units showed the first fully telegraphed swing missing a stationary player in all six cases. The shared AI began winding up at body contact plus 45 units, then stopped moving for the wind-up; its damage check used body contact alone. The next swing landed roughly 2.2 to 2.5 seconds after the first approach. The new Lost Canyon patrol needed an explicit 22-unit reach for its attack to work.

Give ordinary campaign walking enemies a modest eight-unit weapon reach. Use the same reach for starting the wind-up and for resolving its hit. Leave the existing 0.65-second wind-up, dodge/invulnerability, obstacle and height checks, damage values, attack cooldown, specialized enemies, tutorial trial tuning, and other modes intact. Keep the canyon patrol's longer explicit reach. Mirror reach in host enemy snapshots so guest hit checks match. No persistent save data is required.

Verify a stationary player's first ordinary grunt and sentinel swing deals damage at several starting distances, movement or a dodge can avoid it, walls still block it, tutorial trial spawns keep zero reach, old co-op melee behavior remains correct, and static gate passes. This is a focused combat-readability correction, not a campaign balance signoff. Oliver's playtesting will determine whether the new pressure feels right. No custom SFX work.
