# Endless Dungeon expansion — grounded implementation packet

## [Codex | 2026-10-07]

The current Prison Break roguelite has three sections of 11 rooms. A fresh run uses layout revision 8; saved attempts retain their recorded revision. The physical route is connected, but only four ordinary rooms and two crossings lie on each main path. Combat draws exclusively from seven prison foes. The architecture uses a small palette and repeats similar rectangular room shells. The separate Abyssal Descent is unrelated.

For revision 9, extend each section to 16 rooms: add two required combat rooms, a new required broken crossing, an optional elite room and an optional elevated cache. Place this new wing after the current final watch, so the three existing sections and bosses keep their identity. Use section-specific workspaces, obstacles, decoration and clear enemy sockets. The new encounters mix recognizable campaign enemies with the prison roster; their normal AI and special attacks must remain active. Keep existing per-room reward receipts, two-chest policy, persistent gold, scarce healing, class qualification, co-op ownership and old layout hashes.

Color direction: make the new wing readable and vivid. Warm sandstone and teal lamps for cells; copper and bright aqua for underworks; lavender and gold for the escape gate. Keep prison metal and stone structure so color adds orientation without turning the mode into an unrelated place. Visual geometry follows collision; avoid fake platforms, opaque camera obstructions and decorative clutter in fighting lanes.

Verify deterministic generation for many seeds, connected non-overlapping rooms, safe enemy spawns, physical jump reachability, three-section progression, boss exit, one-time rewards, old checkpoint resume, co-op scaling/rewards and real browser rendering in normal and narrow view. Automated checks cannot prove ideal pacing or fun, so report that limit honestly.

## Implemented and verified

Version 2.138.0 gives new attempts revision-9 plans with 16 rooms per section, up from 11. Each section adds a role-specific fight space, a seven-step required crossing, a two-wave guard room, an optional elite store and a seven-step optional cache walk. The new wing alternates between a straight and a side-turn route. The first section has a registry and holding yard, the second a pump house and depot, and the third a beacon workshop and final muster. Earlier prison rooms and bosses remain. Campaign foes now comprise about half the generated ordinary encounter mix, retaining their standard movement, attack and special behavior. The bestiary lists their new location. Architecture uses a brighter sandstone, aqua and lavender progression with wall standards, work fixtures and matching cover. Dungeon falls now have the correct label and recover from the latest cleared room.

Evidence: 1,080 seeded revision-9 plans with no disconnected or overlapping rooms, 8,640 revision-1-to-8 plans byte-identical to the prior implementation, 64,800 safe scheduled spawn positions, 192 browser-controlled successful jumps across the new required and optional routes, a complete three-section clear with two chests per section and Tier-2 unlock, one-time optional elite/cache receipts and resume, 1.6x co-op health with guest reward/checkpoint isolation, and an actual prior-build revision-8 checkpoint loading unchanged in the new build. Representative desktop and phone images were inspected; merged art stays around 35–38k triangles and 9–10 draw calls. Human playtesting remains necessary for run length, difficulty and final visual judgment. No SFX work was added.
