## Implemented in v1.972.0-combat-fairness

- Sporebacks previously triggered TWO death effects: the newer lingering cloud and an older immediate shockwave. Remove the duplicate shockwave. Cloud activation follows a 1.0-second harmless warning; emberling residue waits 0.85 seconds, toxling detonation 0.9 seconds, and skitter residue 0.65 seconds. Residue uses the source height and its own color, with a circular footprint matching its radius.
- Ordinary damaging enemy contact has a 0.65-second preparation and a short active strike window, rather than always-on touch damage. Boss and charger contact only damages during their lunges/charges. Ranged volleys prepare for 0.7 seconds at a committed aim point. Stuns pause hostile attack preparation. Dummy and zero-damage entities do not gain contact warnings.
- Charge/lunge aim locks before execution; cleaves commit a direction at the start. Signature slam, nova, pound, knight, totem, fuse and melee timers have ground warning boundaries/countdowns. Blink stalkers mark a fixed destination before teleporting. Colossus quake waves now wait through the actual 0.7-second warning; Marksman pin warnings show the full 150-unit reach. Zero-damage markers no longer call hurtPlayer.
- At most two thorn patches are authored in suitable named vegetation districts, clear of objectives and with open footing. Late room furnishing does not scatter more. Thorn tangles and healing shrines both lose their translucent square bases; healing remains identifiable by a cross. User clarification about which green effect they meant was requested but not received; neither is claimed to be definitively identified from the verbal description alone.
- Shared rectangle claiming removes overlapping floor/body surfaces in Hollow, Keep, Frost and the four deep-world scenery builders. Outskirts floor tiles now clip partial overlaps, not just fully-contained tiles; tile edge inflation is removed. Collision geometry stays unchanged. This fixes common coplanar floor overlaps, not a claim that every possible prop intersection or transient effect in the game has been visually exhausted.

- All 24 campaign scenes loaded in Chrome without page/world renderer errors, with sampled camera movement and high/low settings. Gameplay screenshots captured; spore and cleave warnings inspected.
- Shared region fixture verifies duplicate and partial-overlap removal, unchanged sampled coverage, and zero positive-area intersections in the resulting rectangles.
- Full campaign bidirectional walking and target reachability fixtures pass. A save made using the pre-change HTML resumes the Hollow charter objective, area and weapon unchanged.
- Tests isolate attack timing from combat balance and suppress experience rewards to avoid pausing in level-up overlays. A full enemy/boss animation-identity pass and crowded-combat tuning remain separate work.
