# Campaign hunt notice — 9 October 2026

Normal-camera screenshots of the Thunder Cliffs tide route showed an unrelated `Combat area` journal notice covering the left part of the playfield while the player jumped. This notice is generated by every authored repeatable patrol camp when the player first enters its radius. Its text is also represented in the optional quest tracker, so a large reading panel is unnecessary at that moment.

Keep the existing hunt flag, kill count, recurring spawns, reward and journal/quest details. Replace only the camp-discovery panel with a short toast naming the target and place, then refresh the tracker. A combat task first discovered by a kill and its completion feedback remain separate. Verify in a muted browser that approaching a camp announces the task, the large journal notice remains closed, progress appears in the tracker, and defeating the target still counts and awards once. This is a presentation fix, not a change in grind balance or enemy behavior.

## [Codex | 2026-10-10] Implemented and checked

Entering an authored repeatable camp now shows a brief optional-hunt toast and refreshes the quest tracker. The existing optional journal entry, kill progression and reward path remain. Muted browser QA confirmed the task appears in the tracker without opening the large journal panel, and defeating the designated foe increments the count. This is a global presentation change for campaign camps; subjective legibility should be checked during normal play.
