# Ruined Keep dungeon: lift winch

The current required lift uses three adjacent weight toggles. The player selects 1 and 3 to total four, then waits for a lift that rises without them. That repeats the same input pattern Oliver found dull in other levels. The stair beside it is deliberately collapsed, so the lift must remain the only complete route for the rescued group and the player.

Replace the weight bank with a hand winch on the lift platform. After freeing the prisoners and reading the confiscated papers, they gather behind the winch. The player grips its handle, walks around the drum, and sees the lift rise in proportion to the turn. One complete turn reaches the upper shelter; reversing or walking back and forth cannot add progress. The operator and prisoners ride together. Letting go or falling off lowers the lift so the player can retry. Once the party reaches shelter, the lift cycles for a late player or co-op partner. Walter's clue, the journal, quest tracker and objective arrow should describe the action plainly without a number puzzle.

Keep the established `kd.lift.open` and `kd.transfer` flags, portal, rescue papers, Sly branch, optional lower works, shards, rewards and boss approach. Old completed saves stay complete; partial `kd.lift.mask` values have no effect. The host owns winch motion and story completion; a co-op guest may operate the handle, the second player sees the platform move, and disconnect releases the grip. Do not start new SFX work.

Verify from normal camera with a real browser: valid proximity and height for grip, partial turn/visible rise, reversing and release/lowering, full turn/transfer, late rider cycle, conversation pause, checkpoint migration, optional lower works, and two-player state. Test the existing route and story checks after updating their assumptions. This pass changes one required dungeon interaction; it does not establish overall campaign quality or human-playtested feel.

## [Codex | 2026-10-11] Implementation and acceptance

Version 2.172.0 installs the winch on the moving platform, with a rotating handle, counterweight and progress markers. Grip requires the prisoner group and confiscated papers; progress follows the operator's angular movement, including guest control through host authority. Reversing does not add progress, and release or fall lowers the lift for a retry. The existing `kd.lift.open` and `kd.transfer` flags remain the completion path, including the late-rider cycle. Prior partial weight masks are ignored, and prior completed flags remain complete.
