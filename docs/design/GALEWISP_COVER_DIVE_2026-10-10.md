# Galewisp dive and overhead cover — grounded plan

The user wants enemies that fight distinctly and remain engaging across the live campaign. A broad natural-combat sweep failed on a Lost Canyon Galewisp. Browser inspection found two different causes. At the test's ground spot beneath the new signal bracket, the wisp follows the player into the bracket footprint; the obstacle lifts it to about 190 units, and it stays directly overhead for twenty seconds without a strike. That is valid cover for the player, but poor enemy movement and presentation. At a nearby open ground spot, the wisp starts a melee wind-up around 72 units above a 44-unit-tall player. Shared melee code stops all flight movement during that wind-up, so the first strikes miss even though the horizontal position and sightline are clear. Some later attacks hit only when random hover timing lowers it.

For Galewisps in campaign adventure, retain the existing warning, dive cooldown, damage, dodge and cover rules. If the target stands under a solid raised platform, the wisp should hover near the platform edge on its approach side until the target moves into the open. It must not hit through the platform or continually push through it. In open ground, the wisp should keep visibly descending through its warned wind-up so its body can reach the player before the strike; the player may still move or dodge away. Do not change other flyers, bosses, tutorial trial difficulty, save fields, lore or custom SFX.

Check a covered target for no damage and no 20-second overhead stall, an uncovered target for a visible warning and timely first hit, and a timed dodge for a miss. Inspect normal-camera views of the covered perch and open dive. Check broad campaign combat and Lost Canyon regressions, old save entry and co-op state. If the broad sweep finds another unrelated invalid spot or failure, report it without suppressing the result. Human playtesting still decides if the attack timing and movement feel good.

## [Codex | 2026-10-10] Implemented and checked

The Galewisp now waits outside a raised platform footprint when its target shelters below it. Its campaign melee starts only with room for its body to clear that platform; during the wind-up it descends to contact height instead of freezing overhead. The small campaign-only melee reach makes wind-up and hit distance agree. The generic role roll had also assigned some Galewisps `exploder`, causing them to self-destruct mid-swoop and sometimes damage the player without completing the dive. Galewisps now retain their signature dive; other flyer role rules, damage, timing, trial tuning and SFX are unchanged.

`scripts/qa-galewisp-cover-dive.cjs` uses the actual canyon bracket and enemy in a fixed browser test slice. It passed 20 successive seeded runs for safe cover, a first open-ground hit with a visible height change, and a timed dodge. Normal-camera covered/open screenshots were inspected. Static gate, Lost Canyon route/checkpoint/climb/save/co-op checks, campaign pursuit, Briar roles and browser enemy navigation passed. One co-op script initially failed during guest bootstrap with missing globals, then passed on its independent rerun. The broad natural-combat sweep still uses a test coordinate beneath the raised bracket for a Galewisp; that is cover, so its expectation of a hit there is invalid. This scoped fix is verified in automation, while combat feel, approach pacing and the full level remain subject to human playtesting.
