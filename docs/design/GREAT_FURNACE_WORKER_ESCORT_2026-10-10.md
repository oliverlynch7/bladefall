# Great Furnace worker escort — grounded change plan

The live player currently opens the worker passage, presses the work and shelter bells in a prescribed order, and watches three workers move safely on their own. The two route choices reverse the order, but the actual action is still two distant button presses. The level already has a credible worker floor, western transport brake, and walkable path to the break-room shelter. Keep that geography and the subsequent cooling and Sunspire-writing objectives.

Make the work bell a start/retry signal after the passage is open. On the quiet branch, the western transport brake must still be stopped first. Three workers walk the existing route at a readable pace; individual HP bars identify who needs protection. Host-owned Legion pursuers arrive from real side approaches in short waves, with fewer enemies if the brake diverted patrols. Some enemies choose workers as targets; others pressure the player. If any worker falls, clear that attempt's attackers, show the retreat, restore the group at the worker floor, and let the player ring again. When all three reach the break room and their attackers are down, complete the existing `gf.evacuated` flag, open the service crossing, and keep progression unchanged. The shelter bell remains a visual destination, not another required button.

Keep completed `gf.evacuated` saves complete and old partial `gf.bells` harmless. If a run reloads mid-escort, let it restart cleanly from the work bell. Co-op shares host-owned worker health, position, enemy HP and completion; either player can defend and both see the bars. No new custom audio. Test an actual enemy damaging a worker, a failure/retry, both route branches, guest combat, prior completed and partial saves, and the cooling/office route. Inspect normal-camera presentation. Automated tests cannot establish that the pacing is fun; Oliver's playtest remains decisive.
