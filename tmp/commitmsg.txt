[autopilot] reaper/Harvested Strength: a soul really does pay for your next skill

The passive's id was read by nothing at all - one of the 46 dead passives in
docs/SKILL_TRIAGE.md section E - even though c2OnKill's reaper branch already
carried a comment saying this is what it does. Armed on a kill there, spent in
useSkill, where the cast is charged to the soul instead of to the pool.

Charged rather than priced at zero on purpose: useSkill reads a zero back from
spendSkillMana as "the cast could not be paid for" and returns, so zeroing the
cost is the implementation that stops the skill firing at all.

Verified with harness/probes/soulfree.probe.js, A/B in one launch, three trials
per half - all six casts paid full price before; free / full price / casts on an
empty bar after. Reaper suite 6 pass / 0 fail either side; passives 86 wired,
38 dead; node tools/gate.js OK.
