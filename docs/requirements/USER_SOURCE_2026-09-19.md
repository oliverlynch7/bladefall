* Use skills.
* Cast magic if it previously could.
* Recognize places and people.
* Perform complex tasks.

But the person’s soul is no longer inside.

They have become:

Hollowed

⸻

13. WHY THEY ARE CALLED HOLLOWED

The term is intentionally simple.

A Hollowed being is literally:

a hollowed version of the person they were.

Their:

* Body remains.
* Memories may remain.
* Habits may remain.
* Skills remain.
* Speech patterns remain.
* Social behavior can be imitated.

But the core spiritual self has been removed.

Their soul exists elsewhere trapped in a timeless black void, untethered from their bodies and unaware of what their bodies are doing, as their soul slowly loses touch with its identity and sanity. 

The name should communicate the tragedy immediately.

Use:

Hollowed

Do not use Hollowborne as the primary term.

“Hollowborne” sounds as though these beings were born as a separate race.

They were not.

They were ordinary living beings who were:

Hollowed

⸻

14. THE HOLLOWING IS NOT ORDINARY NECROMANCY

BLADEFALL includes necromancy as part of its broader magical world, so this distinction matters.

Traditional necromancy concerns:

* Corpses.
* Spirits.
* Raising the dead.
* Manipulating death.

The Hollowing is different.

It does not kill a body and resurrect it.

Instead:

The Hollowing prevents a living body from completing the normal relationship between life and death by removing its soul while preserving the body.

A useful rule:

Necromancy manipulates the dead. The Hollowing prevents the living from fully reaching death in the traditional way. 

There may be shared magical principles, but do not treat them as the same system.

⸻

15. THE HOLLOWED BODY

Once the soul is removed, the body becomes extraordinarily unusual.

The body:

* No longer ages normally.
* Does not die naturally of old age.
* Is no longer affected by normal mortality in the same way.
* Can potentially continue functioning indefinitely.
* Can still be physically destroyed.

This means the Abyss King’s immortality promise was real.

The Hollowed are:

Ageless, not invincible.

The player can physically kill a Hollowed body.

This does not automatically free the soul.

The soul remains trapped inside the Abyssal Void.

⸻

16. THE UNEXPECTED DISCOVERY: CONTROL

The future Abyss King did not originally know that the Hollowing would create controllable bodies.

This discovery is the major turning point in his story.

After the first subjects crossed the Gate:

* Their souls became trapped in the Void.
* Their bodies emerged.
* The future Abyss King realized the bodies could be directly commanded.

He could simply:

speak instructions to them.

The Hollowed body would obey.

They function almost like:

programmable biological robots.

They do not debate.

They do not resist.

They do not reinterpret commands according to personal conscience.

They follow orders:

without question.

This is not traditional magical mind control of an intact conscious person.

The original conscious soul is no longer present inside the body to resist.

The Abyss King is effectively programming an empty but functional living vessel.

⸻

17. HOW PROGRAMMING WORKS

The Abyss King does not need to manually puppet every movement.

He can issue complex standing orders.

Examples:

“Guard this fortress.”

“Report any unusually powerful fighter.”

“Convince your family to enter the Eternal Gate.”

“Scout this region.”

“Do not reveal what happens inside the Gate.”

“Return when you have recruited ten more.”

“Attack anyone who attempts to destroy the Gate.”

The Hollowed then perform those tasks using:

* Their existing knowledge.
* Memories stored physically in the brain.
* Learned behavior.
* Social skills.
* Combat training.
* Magical training.
* Problem-solving ability.

They are functional enough to execute sophisticated instructions.

But:

They cannot choose to disobey.

⸻

18. HOLLOWED CAN MIMIC NORMAL HUMAN INTERACTION

This is essential to the Abyss King’s early rise.

A Hollowed person does not immediately act like a stereotypical zombie.

They can convincingly mimic:

* Conversation.
* Affection.
* Familiar speech.
* Social routines.
* Recognition.
* Humor if instructed/retained.
* Concern.
* Trust.
* Persuasion.

Their body and brain retain enough information to recreate much of how the original person behaved.

But something is wrong.

⸻

19. THE EYES GIVE THEM AWAY

A careful observer can recognize a Hollowed person.

The strongest visual clue is:

their eyes.

The eyes should look:

* Empty.
* Wrong.
* Spiritually vacant.
* Slightly unnatural.
* As though the body is looking at you without someone fully looking through it.

Do not necessarily make every Hollowed eye an exaggerated glowing monster eye.

The effect can be subtle enough that:

* Early loved ones noticed something different.
* They felt that something had changed.
* But they did not yet understand what it meant.

This was especially important during the early Eternal Gate campaign.

Someone might think:

“He looks different.”

but also:

“He walked through a portal and apparently defeated death.”

The miracle was persuasive enough that people overlooked the warning signs.

⸻

20. THE ABYSSAL VOID

The souls removed through the Hollowing do not disappear.

They become trapped within:

the Abyssal Void

This is a supernatural space beyond or beneath ordinary reality.

The trapped souls remain there:

* Untethered from their bodies.
* Unable to return.
* Unable to control what their bodies are doing.
* Collectively imprisoned.

This is the central horror of the Hollowing.

The person is not necessarily gone.

They are:

trapped elsewhere while their body is being used.

⸻

21. WHY HE IS CALLED THE ABYSS KING

The title:

Abyss King

comes from his relationship to:

* The Abyss.
* The Abyssal Void.
* The Gates.
* The enormous population of souls trapped within it.
* His supernatural authority over the Hollowed created through that system.

Eventually, the former lord becomes effectively sovereign over the living bodies connected to the Abyss.

Thus:

The Abyss King

⸻

22. THERE SHOULD BE A WAY TO SEE THE TRAPPED SOULS

At some point in the story, the player should receive credible evidence that the souls truly remain imprisoned.

There should be some form of:

* Spell.
* Magical artifact.
* Ritual.
* Ancient viewing mechanism.
* Soul-sight.
* Void lens.
* Other supernatural method.

that allows someone to temporarily glimpse into the Abyssal Void.

Through this, the player or another character can witness:

* Countless trapped souls.
* Souls corresponding to Hollowed people still walking Aerth.
* The scale of the Abyss King’s crime.

The exact mechanism is open for creative development.

Do not make the souls merely theoretical.

The player should eventually be able to understand:

They are really still in there.

Maybe the player has a powerful moment somewhere during the campaign of looking deeply into the eyes of non combat hostile hollowed npc and seeing a reflection of the many trapped souls in their eyes and having scary and sudden realizations of what that means 

⸻

23. THE MOMENT THE WORLD-DOMINATION PLAN BEGINS

The future Abyss King did not begin with the goal:

“I am going to enslave Aerth.”

The plan forms only after several discoveries come together.

He learns:

1. The Gates actually prevent natural death.
2. Passing through removes the soul.
3. The soulless body can be programmed.
4. Those bodies retain enough social and practical function to operate convincingly.
5. The bodies are ageless.
6. Ian, the last Bladeborn, is dead.
7. No known Bladeborn successor exists.

At that point, an extraordinarily wicked realization occurs:

He possesses a method for manufacturing an obedient, ageless population at exactly the moment Aerth has lost the one kind of protector capable of stopping him.

That is the birth of his conquest plan.

This is the moral turning point.

His immortality project becomes:

a world-domination strategy.

⸻

24. IAN’S ABSENCE CREATES THE OPPORTUNITY

The Abyss King specifically recognizes the historical situation.

Ian had kept Aerth stable and protected.

Ian is now dead.

No known Bladeborn has emerged.

The future Abyss King effectively realizes:

The greatest deterrent against an evil of this scale is gone.

This is his:

golden opportunity.

He believes history has handed him a narrow window in which he can become unstoppable before another Bladeborn ever appears.

⸻

25. THE EARLY STRATEGY IS NOT WAR

The Abyss King is intelligent.

He realizes that immediately attacking kingdoms would:

* Unite enemies against him.
* Reveal the threat too early.
* Force him into conventional war.
* Slow his expansion.

Instead, he chooses:

persuasion

and:

diplomacy

as weapons.

He publicly promotes the Eternal Gates.

⸻

26. THE ETERNAL GATE CAMPAIGN

The message is simple.

Examples:

“Never fear death again.”

“Never abandon your family.”

“Age does not have to take you.”

“Death is no longer inevitable.”

“Remain with those you love.”

“Continue providing for your children forever.”

“Step through the Eternal Gate.”

The campaign is not obviously sinister.

It is:

* Aspirational.
* Hopeful.
* Rational-sounding.
* Emotionally compelling.

The Eternal Gate becomes known as a revolutionary discovery.

⸻

27. THE FIRST HOLLOWED BECOME RECRUITERS

This is the most effective part of the Abyss King’s plan.

Suppose a father enters an Eternal Gate.

He comes out the other side.

He appears alive.

He can still speak.

He remembers his family.

He can return home.

The Abyss King can program him:

“Convince your wife and children to enter the Gate.”

The father returns and says:

“It worked.”

“Look at me.”

“I’m alive.”

“There’s nothing to fear.”

“You can live forever too.”

His family notices:

Something about him seems different.

Especially his eyes.

But:

* He is standing there.
* He knows who they are.
* He remembers things.
* He talks like himself.
* He apparently conquered death.

At the beginning, nobody understands the significance of what they are seeing.

So many follow.

⸻

28. SELF-PROPAGATING RECRUITMENT

The recruitment system becomes exponential.

The basic loop:

1. A person hears about the Eternal Gate.
2. They enter voluntarily.
3. Their soul becomes trapped.
4. Their body becomes Hollowed.
5. The Abyss King programs the body.
6. The Hollowed person returns to trusted people.
7. They persuade those people to enter.
8. Those people become Hollowed.
9. They recruit more.
10. The network grows.

The Abyss King does not have to personally persuade everyone.

His victims become:

the marketers of the system that enslaved them.

This is one of the darkest and most important elements of his rise.

⸻

29. THE HOLLOWED LEGION

As the Hollowed population grows, the Abyss King begins organizing them into:

The Hollowed Legion

Use exactly:

Hollowed Legion

Not:

* Hollow Court.
* Shadowed Legion.
* Hollowborne Legion.

“Legion” is preferable because this is:

* A military force.
* Organized under one supreme will.
* Ultimately controlled by a dictator.

There may still be:

* Generals.
* Captains.
* Knights.
* Mages.
* Scouts.
* Assassins.
* Specialists.
* Commanders.

But they are not an independent council.

The Abyss King is the mind at the top.

⸻

30. HOLLOWED SCOUTS

The Abyss King specifically programs some Hollowed as:

scouts

Their jobs include:

* Watching settlements.
* Tracking resistance.
* Reporting unusually powerful individuals.
* Monitoring rumors.
* Identifying threats.
* Searching for any possible Bladeborn.

This becomes important later when they begin reporting on the player.

⸻

31. THE LEGION CAN CONTAIN MANY TYPES OF BEINGS

The Hollowing is not limited to humans.

Many living beings can potentially be Hollowed.

This allows the army to include:

* Human soldiers.
* Other intelligent peoples.
* Knights.
* Archers.
* Mages.
* Priests.
* Assassins.
* Commanders.
* Beasts.
* Animals.
* Monsters.
* Magical creatures.
* Large creatures.

Do not make the Hollowed Legion visually equivalent to:

“an undead skeleton army.”

The Hollowed are their own supernatural category.

A Hollowed wolf should still look like a wolf.

A Hollowed mage should still look like a mage.

A Hollowed knight should still look like a knight.

All should share subtle signs of Hollowing.

⸻

32. THE SHIFT FROM PERSUASION TO CONQUEST

At first:

“Come willingly and live forever.”

Eventually, the Hollowed Legion becomes large enough that the Abyss King no longer needs cooperation.

The message becomes:

“Enter the Hollow Gate or die.”

This is a major historical transition.

The same object once advertised as the:

Eternal Gate

is increasingly called by the terrified people of Aerth:

the Hollow Gate

⸻

33. HOLLOW GATES

“Hollow Gate” is the name used once the truth becomes known.

This produces a useful propaganda-versus-reality contrast:

The Abyss King’s terminology

Eternal Gate

The world’s later terminology

Hollow Gate

NPC dialogue can naturally reveal historical perspective.

For example:

An old document:

THE ETERNAL GATE — LIFE WITHOUT END

A modern survivor:

“Burn every Hollow Gate you find.”

These are the same portals.

⸻

34. CURRENT PURPOSE OF THE ABYSS KING

By the main campaign, the Abyss King is no longer merely pursuing personal immortality.

He seeks:

domination of Aerth

and the elimination of independent mortal society.

His worldview is essentially:

Mortality is a defect.

And increasingly:

Anyone who refuses his cure is resisting the future.

His strategic reality is:

* Every Hollowed person becomes another subject.
* Another worker.
* Another soldier.
* Another scout.
* Another possible recruiter.

Conquest and Hollowing therefore reinforce one another.

⸻

35. THE CORE CONTRADICTION

The Abyss King’s empire is built around:

freedom from death

at the cost of:

freedom in life.

The thematic core can be summarized as:

The Abyss King offered Aerth eternal life. He simply neglected to mention that the life would no longer belong to them.

Another useful summary:

Salvation became enslavement.

⸻

36. THE STATE OF AERTH WHEN THE PLAYER BEGINS

By the beginning of the main story:

* Ian is long dead.
* No known Bladeborn has replaced him.
* The Hollowed Legion has become extraordinarily powerful.
* The Abyss King’s forces are expanding.
* People understand enough about the Hollow Gates to fear them.
* Settlements are falling.
* Resistance feels hopeless.
* Many people hide.
* Some flee.
* Some surrender.
* Some submit to the Hollowing rather than be killed.
* Some simply resign themselves to what they believe is inevitable.

The emotional state of Aerth is:

the world is giving up.

⸻

37. THE PLAYER

The player does not begin as:

* A famous warrior.
* A celebrated hero.
* A known Bladeborn.
* Someone everyone expects to save the world.

The player begins much more like:

an ordinary person who refuses to accept surrender.

The emotional attitude is essentially:

“Fuck that.”

or in less explicit final dialogue:

“Somebody has to do something.”

The player’s reasoning is:

If everyone is going to die or be Hollowed anyway, I may as well go out swinging

This matters.

The player does not initially act because they know they are destined to win.

They act because:

doing nothing is unacceptable.

Their courage comes before certainty.

⸻

38. THE PLAYER’S BLADEBORN BLOODLINE

Unknown to most or all characters at first:

The player possesses Bladeborn ancestry.

The bloodline has been dormant.

During the story, it begins to awaken.

This is extraordinarily rare.

Do not immediately announce:

“You are the chosen Bladeborn.”

The realization should develop over the campaign.

The player becomes capable of feats that increasingly seem impossible for an ordinary person.

Rumors spread.

Enemies report them.

Eventually the Abyss King becomes interested.

⸻

39. THE ABYSS KING INITIALLY KNOWS NOTHING ABOUT THE PLAYER

At the start:

The Abyss King does not know or care who the player is.

The player is insignificant to him and seen as another citizen who will inevitably walk through the hollow gate

This should change gradually.

His Hollowed scouts begin reporting:

* A fighter surviving encounters they should not survive.
* Legion units being destroyed.
* Commanders falling.
* Resistance growing around this person.
* Increasingly improbable victories.

At first:

annoyance.

Then:

curiosity and nervousness

Then:

concern and rageful anger

Eventually:

fear of a possible Bladeborn.

⸻

40. THE PLAYER BECOMES THE ONLY REAL THREAT

By late game, the Abyss King concludes that:

* Ordinary armies cannot stop him.
* Kingdoms cannot stop him.
* Most organized resistance is broken.
* The player is now the primary meaningful threat to his reign.

Essentially:

The player has become the one person standing between the Abyss King and total victory.

The Abyss King knows the player is coming.

He prepares.

⸻

41. THE FINAL DESTINATION

The player eventually storms:

the Abyss King’s castle

for the final confrontation.

Use the appropriate existing final-castle location from the repository if already established.

Do not rename an existing final castle without instruction.

The Abyss King:

* Knows the player is coming.
* Has received continuous scout reports.
* Has prepared specifically for them.
* Remains confident.
* Still does not completely accept that the player is truly Bladeborn.

⸻

42. THE ABYSS KING’S FINAL DISCOVERY

Shortly before the player reaches him, the Abyss King discovers a dangerous new use for the Hollow Gates.

Because countless Hollowed souls are trapped in the Abyssal Void:

The Void contains an immense reservoir of spiritual energy.

The Abyss King learns that by placing:

part of his own body through a Hollow Gate

while remaining anchored to the physical world, he can:

* Touch the Abyssal Void.
* Draw power from the trapped souls.
* Infuse his physical body with that energy.
* Massively enhance himself.

This is extremely dangerous.

But he does it because the player is coming.

⸻

43. PARTIAL VOID INFUSION

By the start of the final boss fight:

half of the Abyss King’s body has become Void-infused.

That portion of his body is:

* Supernaturally powerful.
* Stronger.
* Faster.
* More durable.
* Altered by Abyssal energy.
* Visually otherworldly.

He is drawing on:

the collective energy of the trapped souls.

Do not imply he permanently destroys those souls by doing this.

He is exploiting and channeling their energy.

They must remain rescuable.

⸻

44. FINAL BOSS — PHASE ONE

Phase One is the partially Void-infused Abyss King.

He should already feel:

* Overwhelming.
* Supernatural.
* Extremely confident.
* Like the strongest opponent encountered so far.

The player nevertheless:

* Survives.
* Matches him.
* Injures him.
* Forces him to fight seriously.

This shocks him.

⸻

45. THE ABYSS KING REALIZES THE TRUTH

During the fight, the Abyss King gradually realizes:

No ordinary person could possibly match this power.

This is where he finally concludes:

the player is Bladeborn.

This should be a major narrative beat during the fight.

Possible emotional progression:

* Confusion.
* Recognition.
* Disbelief.
* Anger.
* Fear.
* Desperation.

His worldview is shaken because he believed the Bladeborn line was effectively gone.

Possible sentiment:

“No…”

“There has been no Bladeborn since Ian.”

“Ian’s bloodline was supposed to be dead.”

Do not necessarily lock exact dialogue yet.

But this is the moment he knows.

⸻

46. THE FULL VOID IS BELIEVED TO BE A DEATH SENTENCE

Normally:

fully entering the Abyssal Void is believed to be a death sentence.

A living being who completely passes into the Void risks:

* Losing their soul permanently.
* Becoming trapped.
* Never returning.
* Being consumed by the forces within.

Even the Abyss King understands this.

Partially entering allowed him to retain a physical anchor.

Fully entering means taking an enormous risk.

⸻

47. THE ABYSS KING TAKES THE LEAP

After realizing he is fighting a Bladeborn, he understands:

His current power may not be enough.

He decides that domination of Aerth is worth risking everything.

Mid-fight:

he jumps completely through the Gate.

For a moment, it may appear that he has effectively killed himself.

But he has become so powerful, so experienced with the Gates, and so supernaturally bound to the Abyss that he manages something believed impossible.

He fights against the forces of the Void.

And:

he returns.

⸻

48. FINAL BOSS — PHASE TWO

The Abyss King emerges:

fully Void-infused.

His entire body now carries the power he previously channeled through only half of himself.

This is the second phase of the final battle.

If the repository already contains a boss/entity called:

The Abyss King, Awakened

that entity should correspond naturally to this final fully Void-infused transformation unless another established implementation makes that impossible.

Phase Two should represent the visual and mechanical apex of the Abyss King’s power.

Potential visual features:

* Entire body partially transformed into Void-like matter.
* Cracks in armor revealing impossible darkness.
* Purple/violet Abyssal energy.
* Space distortion around attacks.
* Parts of the body appearing less physical.
* Fleeting silhouettes or impressions of trapped souls moving within the energy.
* Multiple faint voices layered behind his own.
* Environmental instability as the Abyss presses into reality.

Do not make the battlefield unreadable.

⸻

49. THE PLAYER DEFEATS THE ABYSS KING

The player eventually defeats him.

His death matters magically because:

his soul is the controlling anchor of the Hollowing network.

His death destroys the central authority controlling the Hollowed bodies.

However:

Killing him alone does NOT complete the restoration.

The souls remain trapped inside the Abyssal Void.

The larger binding spell is destabilized but not yet fully destroyed.

⸻

50. THE COLLAPSE OF THE BINDING

When the Abyss King dies:

* The soul-anchor fails.
* The Gate network destabilizes.
* The accumulated Void energy he pulled into reality becomes uncontrollable.
* The dark magical binding begins collapsing.

A massive:

Void rift

opens.

It consumes:

* The player.
* The immediate battlefield.
* Potentially parts of the castle and surrounding environment.

The player is dragged:

into the Abyssal Void.

⸻

51. THE PLAYER INSIDE THE VOID

The Void should feel dramatically different from ordinary Aerth.

Possible qualities:

* Endless darkness.
* Impossible space.
* Floating fragments.
* Violet or Abyssal energy.
* Countless trapped souls.
* Distorted echoes.
* Little sense of conventional distance.

The player can now directly witness the scale of the tragedy.

These are the people the Abyss King Hollowed.

They have been here the entire time.

⸻

52. IAN RETURNS AS A SPIRIT

Inside the Abyssal Void:

Ian’s spirit appears.

Ian is dead.

Do not physically resurrect him into ordinary life.

This is:

* His spirit.
* His soul.
* A final appearance from the previous Bladeborn.

Ian recognizes what the player has become.

He can:

* Guide the player.
* Encourage them.
* Explain what must be done.
* Affirm that the player is truly Bladeborn.
* Express confidence in them.
* Symbolically pass responsibility for Aerth from the old Bladeborn to the new one.

This should be emotionally important.

⸻

53. IAN AND THE PLAYER

Ian represents:

the previous age of protection.

The player represents:

the next.

Ian spent his life protecting Aerth.

His death left a vacuum.

The player rose from an ordinary person who refused to surrender into the new Bladeborn capable of finishing what no one else could.

The emotional handoff should feel earned.

A useful thematic sentiment:

“I kept Aerth safe for as long as I could.”

“Now it is your turn.”

Do not lock exact dialogue without further writing direction.

54. IAN’S BLADE

Inside the Void, Ian gives the player:

Ian’s Blade

Ian’s Blade is:

* The legendary weapon of the previous Bladeborn.
* The strongest known weapon ever created or wielded.
* One of BLADEFALL’s central iconic relics.

Established visual identity for Ian’s Blade should be preserved:

* Massive legendary sword.
* Black iron.
* Tarnished-gold ornamentation.
* Ornate sacred guard.
* Ember-like holy core or internal sacred glow.
* Ancient holy-relic quality.
* Powerful enough to visually communicate that this is not an ordinary weapon.

Ian’s Blade is strongly associated with:

* Bladeborn legacy.
* Protection.
* Legendary power.
* The climax of the story.

⸻

55. IAN’S BLADE DURING THE STORY IS TEMPORARY

Important gameplay constraint:

Completing the campaign must NOT permanently unlock Ian’s Blade for ordinary gameplay.

The story may allow the player to wield Ian’s Blade during the ending sequence.

However:

* The player does not simply receive permanent ownership for beating the campaign.
* The playable/shop version remains an extremely prestigious, overpowered endgame reward.
* Unlocking Ian’s Blade permanently should require a significant grind through the existing shop/progression system.

Do not make campaign completion bypass that progression.

The exact narrative explanation for why the player does not retain the physical Blade after the ending can be handled elegantly later, but do not permanently award it automatically.

⸻

56. THE FINAL VOID CUT

Ian’s Blade gives the player the power required for the story’s final act.

The player channels:

* Their awakened Bladeborn power.
* Ian’s Blade.
* Their own will.
* The inherited legacy of the Bladeborn.

They charge an enormous attack.

Then:

the player cuts through the fabric of the Abyssal Void itself.

This is not merely an attack against an enemy.

It is an attack against:

the magical structure binding the souls.

The player will be met with a considerably difficult mashing section where the player will have to rapidly click the left mouse e button to charge a strong enough attack to cut open the void before a timer runs out and the void becomes too strong like a black hole  and consumes everyone and Aerth in its entirety   

⸻

57. THE BINDING SPELL

The Hollowing exists because of a massive dark-magical binding created through:

* The Abyss King’s soul.
* The Gate rituals.
* The powerful mages/sorcerers who helped establish the system.
* The Abyssal Void.
* The trapped souls.
* The magic connecting bodies to the Void.

Refer to this more precisely as:

the binding

or:

the binding spell

rather than simply calling everything a vague curse.

The player uses Ian’s Blade to:

sever the binding at its source.

This imagery is important.

A Bladeborn wielding the greatest blade in history literally cuts the magical bond enslaving Aerth.

That is highly thematic for BLADEFALL.

⸻

58. THE FINAL CUT FREES THE SOULS

The Abyss King’s death removes his control.

But:

The player’s final Void cut is what actually frees the souls.

When the binding is severed:

* The Abyssal prison breaks.
* The trapped souls are released.
* The connection keeping them separate from their bodies collapses.
* The Hollowing is reversed across Aerth.

⸻

59. WHAT HAPPENS TO LIVING HOLLOWED BODIES

If the Hollowed body still exists:

* The soul returns.
* Free will returns.
* The killed bodies are revived and the person becomes whole again.
* They are no longer programmable.
* The unnatural ageless Hollowed state ends.
* They are restored to genuine life.

This is a joyful restoration.

The people the Abyss King enslaved are finally themselves again.

⸻

60. WHAT HAPPENS TO HOLLOWED BODIES KILLED DURING THE GAME

This is an important part of the ending.

A Hollowed body may have been physically killed:

* By the player.
* In battle.
* During the wars.
* Long before the final confrontation.

Their soul nevertheless remained trapped in the Void.

When the player breaks the binding:

the restoration magic revives/reconstructs their body.

The body returns in:

the healthy state it was in before the person originally passed through the Eternal Gate.

Therefore, the ending can genuinely save:

all of the Hollowed.

Even Hollowed enemies the player had to kill throughout the campaign can ultimately be restored.

This is intentionally a positive, heroic payoff.

The precise magical mechanics of body reconstruction do not need scientific explanation.

It is part of the release of the Hollowing’s binding.

⸻

61. THE HOLLOWED BECOME WHOLE AGAIN

The terminology itself should pay off.

They were:

Hollowed

because their souls were missing.

At the ending:

the souls return.

They become whole.

Their:

* Souls.
* Bodies.
* Free will.
* Identity.

are reunited.

This is the true defeat of the Abyss King.

⸻

62. THE PLAYER ESCAPES THE VOID

The Void-cut does several things simultaneously:

* Destroys the binding.
* Releases the souls.
