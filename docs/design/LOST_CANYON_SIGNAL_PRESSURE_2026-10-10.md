# Lost Canyon signal tower and rescue pressure — grounded plan

Oliver's continuing campaign-quality request calls for purposeful combat, more parkour, readable environmental cause and effect, and fewer stretches that feel empty. In the live Lost Canyon, freeing a cage before cutting the alarm sets `lc.alarm.called`. Two ordinary guards then appear once near the east steps, roughly two thousand units from the south cage. They may never reach the player. The route to the cable is a continuous wide staircase, so its height asks for no jump. The existing freight-store jump route is optional and stays intact.

Make the signal cable a tactical choice. Keep Ruth's two-cage rescue, Captain Ward's entry alternatives, old cage, freight store, prisoner movement, escape gate and all flag/reward IDs. Replace the continuous alarm staircase with a short, irregular sequence of solid scaffold brackets above the existing east yard. The lower yard catches missed jumps and leads back to the first bracket. Use the existing canyon art kit, wood trim and rope/mast details rather than a new low-detail block asset. The broad cable landing remains; this is the sole required moderate climb here, distinct from the longer optional freight climb.

Before an alarm, its signal posts are unlit. Opening either main cage first lights them and starts a brief warning. While the cable remains intact, the host sends small, capped reinforcement pairs from the nearest staffed post to whichever party member is in the holding yard. The pair should wake and fight within normal aggro range, grant ordinary combat rewards, and return after a respite if killed. No waves spawn in the far cave or Ruth's shelter. The posts and winch visibly change when the cable is cut, and no new wave is scheduled; existing guards remain real enemies. Cutting it before opening a cage gives the quiet route. The active objective and arrow point to the cable once the alarm rings.

Do not alter music or custom SFX. Preserve host authority and guest-visible enemy identity, completed/partial old story flags, the quiet route, and the rescue checkpoint. Verification must include actual jump inputs and deliberate miss/recovery, a normal-camera view from below and above, a loud rescue with guards approaching and attacking, reinforcement cap and recurrence, cutting the cable, an old-save reload, the full Lost Canyon route, and a two-page guest/host scenario. Automated checks establish reachability and correctness; Oliver's playtest still determines combat pacing and feel.
