# Repeatable hunt pacing - 2.360.0

User reported too few enemies and long waits, especially Ruined Keep.

NPC-owned repeatable camps now target five enemies instead of three. Safe, supported camp edges are preferred for the two additional enemies, leaving existing formation space. Their per-slot refill is six seconds instead of 25. Finite story/rescue guards, other modes, bosses, trials, quotas, rewards, enemy strength and save schemas are unchanged. Reinforcements retain the existing arrival delay and do not spawn within 110 units of a nearby player. Standing directly over a spawn still deliberately blocks that one spot; stepping away allows it immediately without restarting the timer. Distant or different-storey camps do not refill around absent players.

## Verification

- native.json: foreground Chrome, real WebGL game, isolated in-memory storage seeded with a pre-change level-4 save. All 16 campaign halves build five camp slots with actual supported floors, no solid collision displacement, no nearby NPC overlap and separate bodies after the formation pass. Initial Blackwoods compact fill overlapped a formation destination; filling the camp edges first fixed that and the entire survey was rerun.
- Native Ruined Keep staged diagnostic: accept Grant's actual repeatable quest through its dialogue button; damage/kill handlers exercised on three five-enemy cycles. Clock was advanced explicitly through real patrol tick calls, using the game's spawnEnemy. Zero early refills at 5.99 seconds; five unique supported slot replacements after 6.01 seconds, with formation homes retained. Kill totals 5, 10, 12; no automatic quest payout. Learned an earned passive through the actual choice/confirm menu, then used Grant's actual turn-in button: +260 gold exactly once, round-1 receipt and next offer. Real autosave/persist/continue restored the round and single receipt, with five camp slots. This uses staged positioning, lethal diagnostic damage and clock advancement, not an unassisted combat playthrough or a human pacing verdict.
- Fixture tooling note: the old compact-loot helper panel intercepted the first turn-in click and opened a test chest. The panel was hidden, chest overlay cleared and the real dialogue hand-in repeated successfully. This was isolated fixture UI, not a production quest fix.
- regressions.json lists all passing related checks. Hunts exercise three complete quest rounds for every section, host-only refill, unsafe-floor/blocked-camp rejection, occupied spawn recovery, unique live slots and split-slime offspring ownership. Other regression suites preserve old quests, scene authority and placement behavior.
- Native browser console: no warnings/errors. No real production player saves were modified. Live two-client co-op and a full fresh campaign playthrough were not performed.
