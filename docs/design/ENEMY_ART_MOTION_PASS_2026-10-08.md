# Full enemy appearance and motion pass

## Grounded audit

The earlier v2.128 pass already supplied versioned Blender models for 39 normal/boss appearances and two Frost officers. Ember/Marble Colossi have separate high-detail models and the Hydra is procedural. The seven Prison Break types are **not** in `mob3d.js`; they still use the old immediate-mode block renderer. Existing base GLBs use a shared family palette even where their body shapes differ. `enemy-motion.js` replaces windup/attack/hit for the main cast but retains a simple original idle, gait and death, and only ten enemies have an authored attack profile. `enemy-action-state.js` recognizes a Prison Break attack only when it also carries a beta appearance.

## Design and acceptance

1. Give each active standard appearance a deliberate per-type material palette and improve its signature silhouette/details within browser budgets. Export new versioned GLBs from editable Blender source, preserving prior files as fallbacks. Make seven distinctive Prison Break models on compatible rigs.
2. Replace generic motion for idle, locomotion, windup, strike, recovery, hit and death with anatomy-aware motion: planted alternating steps and torso counter-rotation for humanoids, four-leg patterns and head-led strikes for beasts, wing/hover movement for fliers, distinct wobble/lid/core behavior for small creatures and objects. The attack pose must follow gameplay timing; Prison Break bosses need different poses for different moves.
3. Preserve collision, gameplay timing, enemy IDs, rewards, saves and co-op authority. Keep weapon attachments on their authored bones. Respect reduced motion where relevant.
4. Audit every model/clip for load, finite bone transforms, expected variation, art budget and normal-camera readability. Check representative real encounters, phone rendering, a previous save and several-enemy performance. A browser/automated pass supports correctness; Oliver's playtest decides whether the final motion and art feel right.

The complete appearance coverage target is 39 v2.128 roster types, two Frost officers, two dedicated Colossi, seven Prison Break foes and the three-headed Hydra. The dedicated Colossi and Hydra already have bespoke art; inspect them and refine motion rather than replacing them with the common rig.

## Implemented and checked

Version 2.141.0 exports 48 distinct, editable, versioned Blender appearances: 39 main roster types, two Frost officers and all seven Prison Break types. The two dedicated Colossi retain their bespoke high-detail geometry and receive separate action poses. The procedural Hydra keeps its existing art and now eases each neck/head through a bite rather than jumping to the combat target. Previous GLBs stay available for comparison and rollback. New bestiary portraits are cropped from the current model renders. The [48-appearance contact sheet](../art-validation/enemy-roster-v2141.jpg) is a review artifact, not a substitute for in-game judgment.

The motion layer now builds idle, gait, windup, attack, recovery, hit and death clips from anatomy-specific poses. Every production model has a type-appropriate attack profile. Prison Break and both Colossi use named attack variants to distinguish their different gameplay moves; `prisonFoe` animation state is connected in both regular and beta levels. This does not change damage, collision, rewards or save records. The model budget is 21.13 MB across all 48 on-demand files, with a maximum of 6,360 triangles per standard enemy. Current meshes remain deliberately stylized and rigid-jointed; true soft deformation and foot IK remain outside this pass.

Verification: `scripts/qa-enemy-roster-v2141.cjs` confirms all 48 exports, unique material palettes, base clips and budgets. Browser animation QA loads 50 appearances including both Colossi, checks finite changing idle/moving/attack/hit/death transforms and all named boss variants. Live Prison Break confirms the Bell Keeper switches `Windup_toll` → `Attack_toll` → `Windup_sweep`; standard Briar sets load without missing assets, and the Bell Keeper remains hidden by a solid wall. A 12-enemy, 390px viewport run rendered without errors or overflow (median synchronous render 2.2 ms on the test machine; this is not a device-wide FPS guarantee). The Hydra head travel test confirms an eased first attack frame. A pre-change save loaded with unchanged character data; Prison Break co-op checkpoint/scale/reload checks passed. A fresh human playtest remains useful for judging the actual feel, grip/contact and art quality in long encounters.
