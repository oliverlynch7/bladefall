# Enemy visual overhaul — grounded implementation plan

Oliver authorized the entire enemy roster and animation overhaul, aligned with the completed campaign environment art. The registry contains 40 gameplay types and a marble appearance of the Colossus: 41 appearances. Arena player bots keep the hero renderer.

Author original faceted, vertex-colored Blender models with small skeletons and Idle, Move, Windup, Attack, Hit and Death clips. Design silhouettes around names and home biomes: thorn wildlife, sandstone predators, ruined armor, ice shells, basalt furnaces, void spirits, ivory guardians and corrupted royalty. Include the training dummy, mimic and raid crystal.

Replace only the enemy presentation layer. Preserve the shared prop model loader, all combat logic, dimensions, progression and saves. Associate actors with enemy identity; derive movement from actual position changes because AI does not consistently set velocity. Mirror windup timers and projectile cooldown resets without delaying damage. Bound corpse lifetime and pool reserves. Keep the voxel fallback while assets load or for unknown types.

Verify all registry mappings, animation clips, independent skeletons, asset budgets, pool reuse and real browser combat/rendering. Load a pre-change save. Publish Blender roster previews to the existing phone gallery, bump the game version, append the shipped record and deploy only autopilot-merged. Secondary mode environment and campaign layout redesign remain separate work.

## Completed implementation and validation

Version 1.966.0-enemy-art: 40 gameplay types plus the Marble Colossus appearance. 41 original GLBs, 20,054 triangles across the complete roster, 4,813,828 model bytes, maximum 848 triangles per appearance. All models contain six skeletal clips and ten bones. Blender authoring/render scripts and a reproducible Node validation script are in tools/art.

Real Chrome/WebGL checks covered all 41 mappings, all eight campaign biomes and eight boss appearances, high/low quality, actual hit and kill calls, removed-corpse animation, repeated spawn/kill pool release, missing-asset fallback/recovery, and a pre-change 1.965 save. All completed without runtime errors. Settled samples in Outskirts, Frostfell and Emberdeep were 60 FPS on the development desktop; these samples are not phone hardware benchmarks. Earlier area-entry samples include shader/asset loading and ranged from 30 to 60 FPS.

The phone gallery supports all 41 selections and six clips, tested at 390px width without horizontal overflow. Blender portraits show actual assets under neutral studio lighting. The animated viewer runs one model at a time.

AI, damage, collision dimensions, spawn rules, rewards, saves and campaign layouts are unchanged. Arena player bots and attackable scenery retain their existing specialized renderers. Role/shield indicators and boss wards remain visible. Existing hero, weapon and environment art is outside this enemy pass.

Evidence: docs/art-validation/enemy-*.json. Visuals: public/3d/art-previews/enemies/. Authoring: tools/art/README.md.
