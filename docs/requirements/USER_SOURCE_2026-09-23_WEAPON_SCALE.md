## U186

I'd honestly like them to be slightly oversized for most of the weapons, as long as it doesn't cause a bunch of clipping issues into the character model. I think the blades look cooler and more powerful when they're a bit on the oversized side, but of course, like, still have some variation where daggers are obviously smaller than swords and great swords are far bigger than regular swords, and spell blades maybe have their own place. And I'd also love it too if any weapons have an elemental aspect to them, that they are color-coded for that. We may need to take another look at the elemental system in the game to see if there's potential ways to make that smarter and better.
